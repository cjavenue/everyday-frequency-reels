# FACTS — Sri Lanka's king coconut (thambili)

Every number and claim used in the film, with its source and date. Checked 23 Nov 2026.

| # | Claim in the film | Source (date) | Wording / basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | King coconut is *Cocos nucifera* var. *aurantiaca*, known in Sri Lanka as thambili (තැඹිලි) | Ediriweera, *CORD* 12(2), Coconut Community (Dec 1996); Coconut Research Institute (CRI) papers | "characterized by its bright orange colour … termed Cocos nucifera var. Aurantiaca" |
| F2 | Bright orange husk | as F1; Wijesinghe et al., *CORD* (KC SSR study) | "bright-orange-colour epicarp" |
| F3 | Regarded as native/indigenous to Sri Lanka | Gunathilake & Rathnayake, *CORD* 28(1) (2012), CRI; Attanayake & Fernando (1987) via KC SSR study | "semi-tall variety aurantiaca … which is endemic to Sri Lanka" |
| F4 | Semi-tall palm | Gunathilake & Rathnayake (2012); Perera et al., *CORD* 31(1) (2015) | "The semi tall coconut variety King Coconut" |
| F5 | Sri Lanka's coconuts are grouped into three varieties: tall (Typica), dwarf (Nana) and king (Aurantiaca) | Ekanayake et al., *COCOS* 19 (2010), CRI; Wikipedia "Coconut production in Sri Lanka" | 15 forms under Typica, Nana, Aurantiaca |

| F7 | Sri Lanka exported nearly US$9 million of king coconuts in 2025 | Minister of Plantations Samantha Vidyaratna, *The Morning*, 13 Oct 2026 | "Sri Lanka exported nearly $ 9 million worth of king coconuts in 2025" — attributed on screen as an official figure |
| F8 | About 95% of king coconut exports go to Dubai | CDA Chairman Shantha Ranathunga, Ada Derana / *The Morning*, 29 Jun–1 Jul 2026 | "approximately 95% of Sri Lanka's king coconut exports are directed to the Dubai market" |

## Dropped (could not verify well enough)
- "A king coconut sells for Rs 9,000 abroad" — a single ministerial remark, no price data.
- "Isotonic / same as blood plasma" — common marketing claim; no primary source found.
- Years to first harvest and nuts per palm for king coconut specifically — only figures for other varieties found.
- Fresh king coconut exports down ~33% in 2026 — true (CDA, Jun 2026) but a moving figure; left out to keep the film evergreen.

## Notes
- F7 is an official's figure quoted in the press, not a published customs table; the film says "nearly $9 million" as he did.
- Maps: Natural Earth 1:10m land (public domain). Coastlines only, no borders.
