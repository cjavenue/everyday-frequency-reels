## Dropped (could not be verified well enough)
- "Cinnamon was used by ancient Egyptians" — likely cassia or unknown origin; not specific to Ceylon.
- Health claims (blood sugar, etc.) — trials mixed (Br J Nutr 2012 found no effect on glucose); left out.
- "Sri Lanka is the only producer" — Madagascar and Seychelles also grow C. verum.
