## Facebook description
For a few dawns each winter, sunlight creeps through a slot above the door of Newgrange in Ireland and lights the chamber floor for about 17 minutes. The tomb was built around 3200 BC, older than Stonehenge and the pyramids of Giza.

It isn't alone. Egypt's Great Pyramid lines up with north, south, east and west to within a fifteenth of a degree. El Castillo at Chichén Itzá has four stairways of 91 steps; add the top platform and you get 365, one for each day of the year, and at the equinoxes a serpent of shadow appears on its stairs. Angkor Wat in Cambodia is a map of the cosmos: five towers for the peaks of Mount Meru and a moat for the cosmic ocean.

How did they do it without a compass? Mostly by watching. On the equinox, the tip of a stick's shadow traces an almost perfect east–west line. The real mystery isn't who helped them. It's how patiently they watched the sky.
